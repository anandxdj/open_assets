# Product

## Register

product

## Users

OpenAssets serves creators and small creative teams who repeatedly generate, collect, extract, and organise image assets while working in a browser.

## Product Purpose

Turn browser images and ChatGPT generations into organised, usable OpenAssets assets without forcing users through repetitive downloads, uploads, or chat-history searches.

## Brand Personality

Precise, calm, dependable. The product should feel like a capable creative tool that stays out of the user's way.

## Anti-references

Avoid neon AI-lab styling, harsh brutalism, all-caps monospace interfaces, card-heavy dashboards, decorative animation, and opaque automation.

## Design Principles

- Put the active creative task before product chrome.
- Make automation legible, interruptible, and recoverable.
- Preserve user control over every external action and every upload.
- Keep dense creative workflows calm through strong hierarchy and familiar controls.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG 2.2 AA. Every core workflow must be keyboard-operable, support reduced motion, provide visible focus, use semantic status updates, and never rely on colour alone.
